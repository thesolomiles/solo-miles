# Forest skull concepts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for visual ideation and Blender reference. These images are not game assets or finished 3D models. The final model should simplify fine cracks and foliage and use the project's shared Imphenzia palette.

## A: Antlered woodland giant

Image: a-antlered-woodland-giant.png

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: visual concept reference for a low-poly Blender skull landmark in the game Solo Miles.
Create one landscape concept image with two clearly separated panels: a large cinematic view in the enchanting forest on the left (about 65% of width), and a clean three-quarter asset study of the same skull on a pale muted teal background on the right (about 35%). Keep the skull design consistent between panels.
Style: an achievable low-poly 3D game asset, broad flat-shaded facets, restrained bone-ivory, moss-green, brown and teal palette. The forest has tall slender pines, teal depth haze, warm golden light shafts and a grassy dirt path. Gentle, curious, ancient, enchanting mood.
Left composition: a tiny adult human explorer seen from behind stands near the huge skull for scale, with one small glowing white-blue wisp floating near it. Camera low behind the explorer, looking slightly upward. Skull is roughly five times the person's height, resting naturally on the ground. All horns/tusks fully inside the frame.
Materials: weathered matte ivory bone, recessed open hollow eye sockets and nasal cavity (actual anatomical negative spaces, not black balls), restrained cracks and missing teeth, sparse moss and a few roots through openings. Readable silhouette suitable for modeling.
No horror, no flesh, no blood, no evil expression, no glowing eyes, no elaborate ornament, no text, no labels, no logos, no interface.
Subject direction A: The antlered woodland giant. A deer-inspired elongated skull with broad branching antlers, one antler with a modest broken tip, graceful cheekbones, an absent or partly buried lower jaw. Ancient gentle forest guardian. Simplify antlers into bold low-poly branches; avoid overly intricate twigs.
