# Description

# Blog
Hong Kong gives me anxiety.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/9712ee87-03ed-4835-9f17-0de408e9ad69/IMG_2032.jpg)
It feels a lot like Singapore, except that you jam a lot more people in.
The buildings are taller, the streets are busier, and the drivers.. they are super aggressive. Not a good recipe for a road cyclist.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/b3e915f2-ffa9-463d-beb4-b902fd820783/IMG_1843.jpg)
I came here after winning the Masters Esports Singapore National Championships. I wanted to take a short break.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/9ae17a48-3bc0-47ee-bd5c-e813020b513c/IMG_2023.jpg)
And also meet with my aunt who I haven’t met in more than a decade.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/655c3015-b974-4ed5-a6f0-def5d6d419f2/IMG_1939.jpg)
And also to spend some time with my wife who’s been really supportive in this cycling journey.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/c65b7287-0232-46cb-96a6-a5074d67cecb/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_9.48.49_AM.jpg)
The Dragon’s Back hike, amazing btw.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/fb757aa6-dd84-4605-94e8-c38937e28ebc/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_9.32.34_AM.jpg)
I also shamelessly brought my bike with me. Why not?
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/bf6ab2d8-64c5-4950-8f60-632d37905dd6/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_9.16.16_AM.png)
I learned about Tai Mo Shan while researching about Hong Kong’s viability as a cycling destination. If there’s a mountain, I want to climb it.
I started the ride early to avoid the notorious Hong Kong traffic. It was a good decision.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/3d236f61-2171-4f0e-9412-2d224cc9489e/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_9.58.17_AM.jpg)
Once I got away from the city, the roads became clear. This was a rare sight in Hong Kong.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/8613a92c-0b59-4e61-a2b4-2c73ce19d852/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.01.41_AM.jpg)
The tarmac ended and I found myself in the back roads of rural Hong Kong.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/4f4a91d1-fa53-405c-9091-82593e916615/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_9.56.50_AM.jpg)
Tai Lam Chung Reservoir Dam.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/391ed3bd-dce2-4cf6-a552-b5c2861ccb3d/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.09.17_AM.jpg)
After the reservoir, it was mostly park roads. More like it was built for walking than cycling.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/9769f756-1abc-40dd-842c-2345e8cadc50/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.09.54_AM.jpg)
It was also subjected to the area’s micro-climate.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/8775b39d-3809-40e6-8f8b-eb516737e633/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.13.39_AM.jpg)
Just like in Korea, the elders love a hike. Instead of “Anyeonghasaeyo”, you say “Zou san”, which means “good morning” in Cantonese.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/5c0244ed-0f90-49bc-8bb2-5ea2b0ac628c/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.19.54_AM.jpg)
After what seemed like a really long lead-in to Tai Mo Shan, the dense forest finally gave way.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/bc3fe5ec-5aeb-45ab-b549-6f869cc862bd/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.21.14_AM.jpg)
I could see the mountains.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/3dd40875-c55b-4c89-8082-a5a391bae091/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.21.34_AM.jpg)
..and buffalos. Definitely wasn’t expecting that.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/e866dccf-b99a-4196-a897-fec8cab1c272/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.26.50_AM.jpg)
The finish point is the iconic observatory at the top of the mountain.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/7dc592b5-192e-4660-8ab4-d5fa98d26fdb/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.25.42_AM.jpg)
The final stretches of the road were amazing. It felt like a road to the sky.
![](ef2e34df-57ac-814d-9504-0003b069c2e8/02b46c32-fd34-4a73-af47-909c684dfc89/Screenshot_2026-09-28_at_10.34.07_AM.jpg)
The views from the unguarded edge of the road added to the experience as well.
