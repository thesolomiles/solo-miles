# Solo Miles session routine

- Before inspecting the project or starting work in a new session, read the recent entries in `journal.html`. Follow `.claude/skills/journal/SKILL.md`; use `python3 .claude/skills/journal/scripts/recent.py 5` to catch up, then read older entries relevant to the task when needed.
- Update `journal.html` periodically during a session at meaningful progress or decision milestones, and before ending a session with meaningful work. Do not leave all logging until the end of a long session.
- Keep entries concise and high-level: what changed or was decided, and why. Append to today's entry if it exists; otherwise add a newest-first article and navigation link. Clearly distinguish review findings and proposals from implemented changes.
